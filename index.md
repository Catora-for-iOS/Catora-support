# Catora

**Simple Monthly Budgeting**

Catora is a simple personal budgeting app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

It is designed to help you plan your monthly budget, assign and move money between categories, record income and spending, manage repeating transactions, review spending trends, and understand what is left to spend.

Catora keeps your active budget on your device. JSON backups provide a complete restorable budget copy; CSV import and export exchange transactions and income with spreadsheets.
