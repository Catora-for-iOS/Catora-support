# Catora Support

**Simple Monthly Budgeting**

**Last updated:** 18 October 2026

Thank you for using **Catora**.

Catora is a personal budgeting app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It helps you assign and move money between categories, set savings goals, record income and spending, manage repeating transactions, review spending trends, and understand how much remains available.

## Getting Help

If you have a problem with the app, a question about how something works, or would like to report a bug, please contact:

**Email:** catora.support@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, it can help to include:

- The version of Catora you are using
- Your iPhone, iPad or Mac model
- Your iOS, iPadOS or macOS version
- A description of what you were doing
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened

Please hide account details and other sensitive financial information in screenshots or example files before sending them.

## Getting Started and Learning Help

On your first run, the welcome screen explains Catora, lets you choose **Beginner-friendly text**, and offers **Pink & Lilac**, **Red and Blue**, or **System Default** appearance. It also links to the in-app **Category Budgeting Guide**. You can change the appearance and learning settings later.

Beginner help uses simpler labels and short field tips without changing how the budget works. Tab guides introduce each area once. When beginner help and first-use help boxes are on, key buttons explain themselves before their first use.

On iPhone or iPad, open **Overview ▸ Overview Actions ▸ Learning Help**. On Mac, open **Catora ▸ Settings… ▸ Learning**. There you can turn beginner help and first-use help boxes on or off, open the Category Budgeting Guide, or choose **Show help and tab guides again**. Turning first-use help boxes off and back on also makes the button tips and tab guides available again.

## Backing Up Your Budget

On iPhone or iPad, open the **Overview** ellipsis menu, choose **Manage Data…** under **Data**, then **Backups ▸ Create Backup**. On Mac, use **File ▸ Back Up…**. Save the JSON backup to a file location you choose.

Keep important backup files somewhere safe.

On iPhone or iPad, use **Choose Backup** in **Backups** to select and review a previously saved file. On Mac, use **File ▸ Restore from Backup…**. Review the selected file, then confirm **Restore Backup** to use it.

Restoring replaces the active budget after confirmation. Catora validates the selected file first and keeps the previous active budget as a local automatic recovery snapshot.

JSON backups include goals, their targets and target months, and the balances held in their categories.

## Goals

Open **Goals** (**Savings Goals** with beginner help) from the tab bar or sidebar and choose **Add Goal** (**Add Savings Goal**) to set a name, positive target amount and optional target month. **Add Money** (**Save Money**) moves money from Ready to Assign or another eligible category into an active goal. The goal's progress uses its available category balance for the selected budget month; the suggested monthly amount is guidance and does not move money automatically.

Tap or click a goal to edit or archive it. Archiving returns unused goal money to Ready to Assign and keeps its history. You can unarchive it later. Only a goal with no money or activity can be permanently deleted. See the [User Guide](guide.html) for more detail.

## CSV Import and Export

On iPhone or iPad, open **Overview ▸ ellipsis ▸ Manage Data…** (under **Data**), then **CSV Import & Export**. On Mac, use **File ▸ Import CSV…** or **File ▸ Export CSV…**.

CSV exchanges transactions and income with spreadsheet software. Import reviews the selected file before appending approved rows; it does not replace your budget. Resolve invalid rows and category choices before importing. Possible duplicates are excluded unless you explicitly include them. Cancelling or a failed validation or save leaves the existing budget unchanged.

CSV does not preserve goals or their targets and target months, monthly assignments, transfers, repeating rules, category order or archive state, or app settings. Use a JSON backup for a complete restorable budget copy. CSV files can contain sensitive financial details: Catora reads or writes only selected files and does not upload them to a developer server. Keep exports secure.

The [User Guide](guide.html) explains the CSV format and review steps.

## Starting Again

Open **Reset…** from the **Overview** ellipsis menu on iPhone or iPad, or **File ▸ Reset Budget…** on Mac. Reset offers two ways to begin again: **Start Fresh** creates the normal starter categories with no money assigned, while **Start Again with Sample Data** creates a small example budget.

The app asks for confirmation before replacing the active budget. Reset removes Catora's local automatic recovery versions; the app warns if cleanup cannot be completed. Separately saved backups and CSV exports remain until you delete them from their saved location.

After a complete reset, the welcome screen appears again and the tab guides can be shown again. Appearance and App Lock settings remain unchanged. Use **Learning Help** to replay first-use button tips.

## Currency

Catora uses the currency and number formatting associated with your device's regional settings.

If the currency shown in the app is not what you expect, check the **Language & Region** settings on your device.

## Device Authentication

If App Lock is enabled, Catora uses Apple's system authentication, which may use Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode on iPhone and iPad, or available Touch ID or the Mac login password on macOS, to protect access to the app.

Authentication is handled by the operating system. Catora receives only whether it succeeded or failed and does not receive or store biometric data, the device passcode or the Mac login password.
