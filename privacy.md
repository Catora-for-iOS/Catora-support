# Catora Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Catora — Simple Monthly Budgeting** handles your information.

## Your Budget Data

Catora is designed to keep your budgeting information on your device. It does not automatically sync the active budget between devices; you can transfer a saved JSON backup yourself.

Information you enter into the app may include:

- Budget categories, monthly assignments and transfers between categories
- Transactions
- Income
- Repeating transactions
- Payees, income sources and notes associated with your financial records
- App appearance and preference settings

This information is used only to provide the budgeting features of the app.

## Data Collection

Catora does not send your personal or financial information to the developer or a developer-operated server.

The app does not use advertising or tracking technologies.

The app does not use your information for marketing or advertising purposes.

## Local Storage and Recovery Copies

Catora stores its active budget in the app's local storage. To help recover safely from interrupted changes or an unreadable file, Catora keeps local automatic recovery snapshots, including a previous local copy, and may preserve an unreadable local file.

Reset replaces the active budget with either an empty starter budget or sample data and removes Catora's local automatic recovery versions. If recovery-copy cleanup cannot be completed, the app warns you. Backup and CSV files saved separately remain until you delete them from their saved location or storage provider.

## Backups

On iPhone and iPad, open the **Overview** ellipsis menu, choose **Manage Data…** under **Data**, then **Backups** and **Create Backup**. On Mac, choose **File ▸ Back Up…**.

A portable backup is only exported or shared when you choose to do so. You control where it is stored using the file options provided by iOS, iPadOS or macOS. This may include local storage, iCloud or another storage provider available on your device.

The developer does not automatically receive copies of your backups.

To restore on iPhone or iPad, use **Choose Backup** in **Backups**. On Mac, choose **File ▸ Restore from Backup…**. The app reads only the file you select, lets you review its contents, and restores it only after you confirm **Restore Backup**.

You are responsible for keeping any backup files you create secure.

## CSV Import and Export

Catora can import and export transactions and income as CSV files. It reads or writes only files you select and does not upload their contents to the developer or a developer-operated server. Your chosen storage provider may store or sync those files according to its own settings and privacy practices.

CSV files may contain dates, payees or income sources, categories, amounts and notes, including sensitive financial information. Before importing, Catora lets you review rows, resolve category choices and choose whether to include possible duplicates. Approved rows are appended to the existing budget. CSV does not include monthly assignments, transfers, repeating-transaction rules, category order or archive state, or app settings; use JSON backups for a complete restorable budget copy.

Keep CSV exports secure and delete them from their saved location when no longer needed.

## Device Authentication

If App Lock is enabled, Apple's system authentication handles access using available biometrics or the device passcode on iPhone and iPad, and available Touch ID or the Mac login password on macOS.

Catora does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial recognition data, device passcode, Mac login password, or other biometric information.

The app only receives confirmation that authentication succeeded or failed.

## Internet Access

Opening Catora's privacy, support or user-guide links opens hosted web pages. Those requests are handled by GitHub Pages and your browser according to their own privacy practices; Catora does not include your budget data in them. If you contact support by email, the developer receives the information you choose to send. Avoid sending sensitive financial details.

The core budgeting features of Catora do not require your financial information to be uploaded to an online account or server.

## Financial Information

Catora is a personal budgeting and record-keeping tool.

It is not a bank, financial institution, investment service, accounting service, or financial adviser.

The app does not connect to your bank account.

## Children's Privacy

Catora is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the app's budgeting information is stored locally and is not collected by the developer, the developer does not knowingly maintain personal information about children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Catora's features or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
